Why BNL-OS

Built for the edge,
not the cloud.

Every decision in BNL-OS was made with constrained hardware and production reliability in mind.

⚙️

Yocto-native

Every layer, recipe, and BSP is version-controlled with BitBake. Fully reproducible builds across machines.

🔒

Security-hardened

Minimal attack surface. No unnecessary daemons. Read-only rootfs by default with dm-verity integrity checks.

⚡

Fast boot

Optimized systemd unit ordering and suppressed unused services yield sub-3-second boot on target hardware.

🔄

A/B OTA updates

Dual-bank partition layout with atomic flipping. Failed updates automatically roll back to the previous good slot.

📦

Minimal footprint

Under 50 MB rootfs. Runs on hardware with as little as 128 MB RAM, suitable for deeply embedded targets.

🧩

Modular layers

Compose your image by including only the Yocto layers you need — networking, app runtime, BSP, crypto and more.
